About the Troop

What Scouts Actually Do Here

Scouts BSA is the traditional Scouting program: youth-planned outdoor activities, real responsibility, and merit badges earned along the way — not handed out. Scouts explore hiking, camping, and canoeing while working toward Scouting's highest rank, Eagle Scout.

Scouts who stick with the troop past three years are far more likely to make Eagle, which is exactly why we build a program worth sticking around for.

Leadership Counts

  • Senior Patrol Leader

  • Patrol Leader

  • Quartermaster

Every Scout gets a real shot at running things — the adults are there to advise, not to lead.

Outdoor Skills

Canoe trails, hiking, and challenges Scouts first thought were beyond them. There's no better teacher than the outdoors.

Service & Growth

Scouting channels the physical and emotional growth of these years into service projects and real community engagement.

Timeless Values

The Scout Oath and Scout Law build academic skills, self-confidence, ethics, and citizenship that carry into adulthood.

Troop History

Troop 279 was established on September 1, 1951, and has been registered continuously ever since. Our first charter was signed on November 10, 1951.
